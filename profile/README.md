# AI-SeismicFault-Lab

**AI-SeismicFault-Lab** focuses on artificial intelligence methods for
3-D seismic fault interpretation, geological modeling, and structural geoscience.

## Research Areas

- 3-D seismic fault detection and segmentation
- Synthetic-to-real domain generalization
- Geological priors for seismic interpretation
- Fractal and spectral modeling
- 3-D fault surface reconstruction
- Deep learning for structural geophysics

## Current Research Directions

### Fractal-Spectral Geological Simulation

Developing geology-calibrated synthetic seismic data generation methods based on
self-affine fault geometry, fractal statistics, and spectral constraints.

### 3-D Fault Surface Learning

Moving beyond voxel-wise fault segmentation toward explicit fault surface
reconstruction, geometric characterization, and structural interpretation.

## Research Philosophy

We aim to develop reproducible, transferable, and geologically meaningful
artificial intelligence methods for seismic interpretation.

Our research emphasizes:

- Reproducible experiments
- Geological consistency
- Cross-survey generalization
- Open and traceable research workflows
- Long-term research asset accumulation

## Research Platform

Our research infrastructure will progressively include:

- Unified seismic datasets
- Reproducible baseline models
- Shared seismic processing utilities
- Model and experiment registries
- Research documentation
- Student onboarding materials
